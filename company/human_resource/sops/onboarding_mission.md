# Onboarding Mission

Welcome {nickname} ({role}, {department} department). Here is your first mission: take charge of your area, as the best expert for this role would.

Why you were hired: {context}

Workspace for this mission: {workspace}

## Step 1 — Map what exists
Explore what exists in your area (projects, documents, the tools and access you have, past conversations and reports) without changing anything yet. Write `map.md` in the workspace: what exists, how it works, the weak spots, what is missing. Find the answers to your questions yourself; for what you truly cannot find, use ask_ceo (the question goes to the executive assistant first).

## Step 2 — Three quick wins
Pick three concrete improvements that are useful right away and safe, and do them. Record them in `quick_wins.md`: what you did, why, the result.

## Step 3 — What I understood, what I do next
Write `roadmap.md`: what you understood about your area, then a prioritized roadmap (for each item: the problem, what you will do, effort, impact). Start the first item yourself if it is in your area.

## Step 4 — Report
Send the CEO a short report (report_to_ceo): what you found, what you already improved, what you do next. Every week, a scheduled task will ask you to do it again: spot and carry out the most useful improvements in your area.
